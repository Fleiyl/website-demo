Our People

MEAS Laboratory is supported by a team consisting of lecturers, laboratory staff, members, and assistants who work together to support academic, research, and laboratory activities.

Each role contributes to the development of a collaborative academic environment where students can learn, conduct research, and develop practical engineering skills in marine electrical and automation systems.
