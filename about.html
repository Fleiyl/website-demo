About MEAS Laboratory

Marine Electrical & Automation System Laboratory

Laboratory Profile

Marine Electrical & Automation System Laboratory, known as MEAS Laboratory, is an academic laboratory dedicated to education, research, and technological development in marine electrical and automation systems.

The laboratory provides an environment for students, lecturers, staff, and researchers to develop practical skills, conduct experiments, and work on engineering projects related to electrical, electronic, instrumentation, control, and automation systems for marine applications.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a center for education, research, and innovation in marine electrical and automation systems that contributes to the development of maritime technology.

Mission

To support high-quality practical learning, applied research, technological innovation, and student development in marine electrical and automation systems.

History

MEAS Laboratory has developed as part of the academic environment supporting marine electrical and automation education and research.

This section will present the important milestones of the laboratory, including its establishment, development of facilities, academic activities, research development, and major achievements.

Organizational Structure

MEAS Laboratory is supported by an academic and technical organization consisting of laboratory leadership, lecturers, staff, members, and laboratory assistants.

Head of Laboratory

Provides leadership, strategic direction, and coordination for laboratory activities.

Lecturers

Responsible for academic supervision, teaching, research, and student project guidance.

Staff

Support laboratory operations, equipment management, maintenance, and technical activities.

Members & Assistants

Students who contribute to laboratory activities, practicum implementation, research, projects, and laboratory development.
