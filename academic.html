Academic Activities

MEAS Laboratory supports academic activities that connect theoretical knowledge with practical implementation in marine electrical and automation systems.

Through laboratory practicum, related courses, and final project or thesis activities, students are given opportunities to develop technical skills, conduct experiments, analyze engineering systems, and apply electrical and automation concepts to marine applications.

Practicum

MEAS Laboratory provides practical learning activities designed to strengthen students' understanding of marine electrical and automation systems.

Basic Electronics

Practical activities covering basic electronic components, circuits, measurements, and fundamental electronic systems.

Marine Electrical Systems

Study and practical implementation of electrical systems used in marine applications, including power distribution and electrical equipment.

Automation & Control

Practical learning related to control, instrumentation, automation, sensors, actuators, and industrial control systems.

System Simulation

Simulation-based learning using engineering software to analyze and evaluate electrical and automation system behavior.

Courses

MEAS supports courses related to marine electrical engineering, electronics, instrumentation, control, and automation. These courses provide the theoretical foundation required for understanding electrical and automation systems on ships.

Course information, learning materials, practicum schedules, and related academic resources can be provided through this section.
