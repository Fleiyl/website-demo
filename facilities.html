Facilities

Laboratory Facilities, Equipment, and Engineering Software

Laboratory Facilities

MEAS Laboratory provides facilities that support practical learning, research, experimentation, and engineering project development in marine electrical and automation systems.

The facilities are designed to allow students and researchers to work with electrical systems, electronic circuits, instrumentation, control systems, and marine automation technologies.

Laboratory Facilities

The laboratory provides dedicated areas and supporting infrastructure for academic, research, and technical activities.

Electrical Laboratory

A dedicated area for studying and experimenting with electrical power systems, electrical components, measurements, and marine electrical applications.

Electronics Laboratory

A practical workspace for electronic circuit development, testing, measurements, embedded systems, and electronic applications.

Automation Laboratory

Facilities for studying control, instrumentation, automation, sensors, actuators, and monitoring systems.

Research Workspace

A workspace supporting student final projects, research activities, prototype development, testing, and experimentation.

Equipment

MEAS Laboratory is equipped with various instruments and devices used for electrical measurements, electronics, instrumentation, control, and automation experiments.

Equipment information can be organized based on its application, specification, availability, and laboratory location.

View Equipment

Measurement Instruments

Instruments used for electrical, electronic, and system measurements during laboratory experiments and research.

Electrical Equipment

Equipment supporting electrical power, distribution, testing, and marine electrical system experiments.

Control Equipment

Devices used for control system, automation, instrumentation, and actuator experiments.

Electronics Equipment

Electronic components, development boards, interfaces, and supporting equipment for circuit and embedded system development.

Software

Engineering software is used to support simulation, modeling, analysis, programming, system design, and research activities within MEAS Laboratory.

Software resources allow students and researchers to analyze electrical and automation systems before implementing them in physical laboratory environments.

MATLAB & Simulink

Used for mathematical modeling, simulation, system analysis, control development, and engineering data processing.

ETAP

Used for electrical power system modeling, analysis, load flow, short-circuit, and system studies.

Engineering Design Tools

Software tools supporting electrical, electronic, automation, and engineering system design and documentation.

Programming & Development

Programming environments used for embedded systems, data acquisition, automation, and experimental system development.

Facility Usage

MEAS facilities can support various academic and engineering activities, including laboratory practicum, student projects, final projects, research, prototyping, testing, and system development.

Access and usage of laboratory facilities are managed according to applicable laboratory procedures and regulations.
