Research

Research, Innovation, and Technology Development in Marine Electrical & Automation Systems

Research at MEAS

MEAS Laboratory develops research activities focused on marine electrical, electronics, instrumentation, control, and automation systems.

Research activities are directed toward solving practical engineering problems and developing technologies that can support safer, more efficient, reliable, and intelligent marine systems.

Research Areas

Research at MEAS covers several interconnected fields within marine electrical and automation engineering.

Marine Electrical Systems

Research on shipboard electrical power systems, power distribution, electrical equipment, protection, and system performance.

Marine Automation

Development and analysis of automation, control, monitoring, and supervisory systems for marine applications.

Instrumentation

Research involving sensors, measurement systems, data acquisition, monitoring, and instrumentation for marine systems.

Marine Electronics

Development of electronic systems, embedded systems, communication interfaces, and electronic devices for marine applications.

Control Systems

Research on modeling, simulation, control strategies, and intelligent control systems for marine engineering.

Digital Marine Systems

Application of digital technologies, data processing, connectivity, and intelligent systems to support modern marine operations.

Research Roadmap

The MEAS research roadmap provides a long-term direction for developing research capabilities and technological innovation in marine electrical and automation systems.

The roadmap connects fundamental studies, applied research, prototype development, system integration, testing, and implementation in marine environments.
