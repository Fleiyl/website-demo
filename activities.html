Laboratory Activities

MEAS Laboratory conducts various academic, research, technical, and organizational activities throughout the academic year.

These activities provide opportunities for students, lecturers, staff, and laboratory members to collaborate, develop technical skills, share knowledge, and contribute to the development of marine electrical and automation systems.

Practicum Activities

Practicum activities are an important part of MEAS Laboratory's academic contribution. Students apply theoretical concepts through direct experiments and measurements.

Practicum activities cover areas such as electronics, electrical systems, instrumentation, control, automation, and system simulation.

View Academic Activities

Electronics Practicum

Practical experiments involving electronic components, circuits, measurements, and basic electronic systems.

Electrical Practicum

Practical activities related to electrical systems, measurements, equipment, and marine applications.

Automation Practicum

Experiments involving sensors, actuators, control systems, automation, and monitoring.

Simulation Practicum

Computer-based modeling and simulation used to analyze engineering system behavior.
